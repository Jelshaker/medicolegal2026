## **Lui v. Thomas / Diagnostic Imaging NW (Over the pond)**

**Multnomah County Circuit Court, Oregon, United States**  
**11-day jury trial — Verdict returned November 2026 ($7.8 million)**

### Facts and imaging

Wendi K. Lui, a 51-year-old non-smoker with dense breast tissue and a family history of breast cancer, underwent annual supplemental screening breast MRI at Legacy Good Samaritan Medical Center, Portland, Oregon.

- **2018 breast MRI:** A ~1 cm pulmonary mass was visible within the imaged thoracic field of view but went unreported.
- **October 2019 breast MRI:** The mass had grown to ~2 cm. The reporting radiologist, Dr Kari A. Thomas (Diagnostic Imaging NW), did not report it as suspicious, later testifying that she believed the focus represented an imaging artefact rather than a true pulmonary lesion.
- **2020 diagnosis:** By the time the mass was identified, it had tripled in volume and metastasised to four lymph nodes. Mrs Lui was diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer and died in 2023.

### Outcome and significance

Mrs Lui's estate and husband (who declined confidential settlement offers) pursued the claim to trial. The jury awarded **$7.8 million** against the radiologist and her practice group.

For UK radiologists, the verdict illustrates the familiar hazard of "tunnel vision" on organ-specific protocols: structures included in the field of view—such as the lung bases, mediastinum, and chest wall on breast MRI—must be systematically reviewed rather than assumed to be artefactual.
