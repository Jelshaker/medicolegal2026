## **Oh v. Jeffries, M.D., et al. (Over the pond)**

**Fulton County State Court, Georgia, United States — Case No. 20EV002508**  
**Verdict delivered: 4 August 2026 ($15 million)**

### Facts and procedure

Byung "Ben" Oh, a 77-year-old retired veteran, was admitted to WellStar Atlanta Medical Center in April 2018 after a fall and underwent spine surgery on 4 May 2018. Post-operatively he developed a **chyle leak**, abdominal distension, and reduced urine output.

- **Interventional error:** Interventional radiologist Dr Barry Jeffries (Diagnostic Imaging Specialists) was consulted to insert a **suprapubic catheter**, but positioned the catheter **outside the bladder**.
- **Post-procedural failure:** Ward staff mistook the copious milky-white drainage for urine. Over 11 days, approximately **87 litres** of chylous fluid drained from Mr Oh's abdomen, leading to severe nutrient and fluid depletion, septic shock, multiorgan failure, and death on 21 July 2018.

### Outcome and significance

The Fulton County jury awarded **$15 million** ($13m for full value of life; $2m for conscious pain and suffering), apportioning **32.5% fault** to the interventional radiologist and practice group and **67.5% fault** to the attending physician and hospital group (clearing a nephrologist and urologist). For UK interventionalists, it underscores the need to confirm intravesical catheter position fluoroscopically or sonographically and to escalate unexpected drain output immediately.
